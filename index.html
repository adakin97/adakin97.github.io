Damage Maps

2026

problem: assessing structural damage in conflict zones is slow, dangerous, and often impossible on the ground. Cloud cover defeats optical satellites.

built: a damage-detection pipeline on Google Earth Engine using Sentinel-1 SAR radar, which sees through cloud and darkness. Applies the PWTT (pixel-wise t-test) change-detection algorithm developed at UCL to radar time series, wrapped in my own ingestion, processing, and visualisation pipeline. A second stage polls NASA FIRMS/VIIRS thermal-anomaly data (~3h latency) and queues SAR confirmation automatically, so new strikes are detected and verified without manual intervention.

outcome: damage assessments across seven areas including Bakhmut, Tehran, Natanz, and Al Udeid. Now extending into a displacement-pressure model combining population, nighttime-lights, and vegetation data.
